## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, mission-critical operational interface for Indonesian B2B occupational safety (K3), statutory compliance, and industrial training oversight. The system balances high-level enterprise credibility with the rigor of physical industrial audits, drawing cues from command-center aesthetics, structural architectural drafting, and regulatory registry interfaces.

The emotional baseline is uncompromising precision, certified accountability, and institutional trust. Rather than soft decorative surfaces, the visual language relies on absolute 90-degree geometric profiles, high-contrast monochrome structural planes, deep maritime slate foundations, and high-visibility industrial green cues (Kemnaker/BNSP standard adherence). Visual complexity is minimized to facilitate zero-latency data triage across complex compliance checklists, regional schedules, and audit registries.

## Colors

The palette operates strictly within high-contrast technical parameters. The foundational background rests at `#070D18` (Regulatory Slate 900) paired with `#0F2038` (Primary Command Slate) to maintain extreme legibility during extended operational sessions and audits. 

- **Primary Command Slate (`#0F2038`)**: Anchors structural surfaces, control toolbars, active headers, and credential panels.
- **Safety Emerald (`#0D7A5F` / `#10B981`)**: Utilized exclusively for certified validation states, official Kemnaker/BNSP credentials, pass-status tracking, and high-priority primary triggers.
- **Caution Amber (`#D97706`)**: Reserved strictly for pending audits, statutory expiration notices, regulatory warning indicators, and priority triage tags.
- **WhatsApp Action (`#25D366`)**: Applied strictly to instant enterprise liaison nodes and fast-track consultation triggers, framed by sharp neutral containers to avoid visual pollution.
- **Border Grids (`#1E324E`)**: Provides definitive technical containment lines that visually segment dense tabular and metadata layouts without drop-shadow reliance.

## Typography

The typographic system utilizes a dual-engine architecture: `Space Grotesk` dictates titles, technical labels, and credential banners, lending a calibrated, industrial-grade tone. `IBM Plex Sans` powers body descriptions, legal disclaimers, training syllabi, and dense data tables with exceptional tabular alignment and sustained legibility.

All headings and micro-labels demand rigid casing rules. Status flags, accreditation IDs, and metric labels must render in uppercase via `label-caps` with strict tabular spacing. Mobile adaptations preserve structural alignment while reducing header scale to prevent premature word wrapping in administrative panels.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on a standard 12-column architectural grid on desktop devices ($1280\text{px}+$ breakpoint) and a disciplined 4-column system on mobile devices ($360\text{px} - 767\text{px}$). 

Grid parameters:
- **Baseline Rhythm**: Built on an absolute 4px/8px modular base. Elements, dividers, and component interiors lock directly to these coordinates without fractional pixel variances.
- **Table Density**: Industrial listings, participant rosters, and city availability matrixes rely on dense horizontal stripes with 8px internal padding on cell axes to maximize viewport economy.
- **Structural Panels**: Container borders line up on identical horizontal and vertical planes, emulating structural framing. Full-width horizontal baseline rules separate executive summary sections from granular schedules.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids blurred drop shadows and skeuomorphic casting. Elevation is communicated through three precise flat-plane techniques:

1. **Tonal Boundary Layering**: Elevation is established by stepping dark backgrounds from base slate (`#070D18`) to workspace layer (`#0B1526`) to interaction panel (`#0F2038`).
2. **Crisp 1px Hard Outlines**: All interactive cards, modal screens, and data blocks utilize high-contrast 1px solid borders (`#1E324E`). Hover states do not lift through shadows; they brighten their structural perimeter to `#0D7A5F` or `#38BDF8`.
3. **Hard Inset Offsets**: When distinct separation is required for selected states or high-urgency compliance alerts, a 2px interior border or an unblurred 2px solid offset indicator is deployed directly along the left perimeter edge.

## Shapes

The roundedness level is strictly set to `0` (Sharp). No corner radii are permitted across any layer, button, input field, credential badge, dropdown, or modal container.

Every element manifests as an uncompromising, razor-sharp rectilinear form. This strict geometry reinforces the uncompromising nature of safety standards, statutory compliance audits, and enterprise accountability. Segmented tabs, button groups, and badge indicators dock flush against adjacent boundaries, creating clean, interconnected grid clusters.

## Components

### Action Triggers (Buttons)
- **Primary Operational Button**: Background `#0D7A5F`, text `#FFFFFF`, font `Space Grotesk` Medium, uppercase, 0px border radius, 12px 24px padding. Hover: Background `#10B981`. Active: Inset 2px solid `#070D18`.
- **Secondary Command Button**: Background `#0F2038`, border 1px solid `#1E324E`, text `#F1F5F9`. Hover: Border `#94A3B8`, background `#142338`.
- **WhatsApp Direct Trigger**: Background `#070D18`, border 1px solid `#25D366`, text `#25D366`, displaying the official instant inquiry icon with 0px corner geometry. Hover: Background `#25D366`, text `#070D18`.

### Accreditation Badges & Credential Chips
- Rendered in full uppercase `Space Grotesk` (`label-caps`), zero radius, 4px 8px padding.
- **Kemnaker RI Certified**: Background `#0B1526`, border 1px solid `#0D7A5F`, text `#10B981`.
- **BNSP Accredited**: Background `#0B1526`, border 1px solid `#38BDF8`, text `#38BDF8`.
- **Statutory Warning / Expiring**: Background `#0B1526`, border 1px solid `#D97706`, text `#F59E0B`.

### Form Fields & City Selectors
- Background `#0B1526`, border 1px solid `#1E324E`, text `#F1F5F9`, font `IBM Plex Sans`. 0px border radius.
- Height standard: 44px. Focus state: Border 1px solid `#0D7A5F` with immediate no-offset outline.
- Interactive City Selectors: Designed as a dense horizontal segmented selector or rigid tabular list with explicit checkmark symbols instead of rounded bubbles.

### Compliance Cards & Syllabus Containers
- Background `#0B1526`, border 1px solid `#1E324E`. Top border features an optional 2px accent rule in `#0D7A5F` or `#0F2038`.
- Inside paddings: 24px on desktop, 16px on mobile. No shadow under any interaction condition.

### Data Tables & Roster Lists
- Alternating row fills: `#070D18` and `#0B1526`. Dividers: 1px solid `#142338`.
- Column headers: Uppercase `Space Grotesk` (`label-caps`), background `#0F2038`, text `#94A3B8`, cell vertical alignment center.

### Selection Controls
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Absolute square frames (16x16px), 0px radius, 1px solid border `#1E324E`. Selected: Solid `#0D7A5F` fill with razor-sharp internal checkmark glyph or square center block.
